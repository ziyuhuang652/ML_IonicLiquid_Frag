\section*{BF$_4$ Geometry Benchmark for MACE-Optimized EMI--BF$_4$}

As a fast geometry-level benchmark, the neutral EMI--BF$_4$ starting structure
used in the wall-collision workflow was optimized with MACE-medium and
MACE-POLAR-1 and compared with the DFT-optimized and crystallographic
geometries reported by Laws et al. The most informative discrepancy is the
anion substructure. In the published DFT geometry, three B--F distances are
near \SI{1.36}{\text{\AA}}, whereas the fourth distance is elongated to
\SI{1.703}{\text{\AA}}. The crystallographic reference, by contrast, reports
four similar B--F distances near \SIrange{1.38}{1.40}{\text{\AA}}. Both
MACE-medium and MACE-POLAR-1 relax the BF$_4$ unit toward a chemically
reasonable near-tetrahedral anion, with all four B--F distances between
approximately \SIrange{1.40}{1.44}{\text{\AA}}. Thus, although the foundation
models do not reproduce the asymmetric DFT-optimized BF$_4$ distortion, they
are closer to experiment for the anion bond-length substructure, especially for
the anomalous B$_1$--F$_4$ coordinate. This supports using the MACE models as
rapid geometry and trajectory generators while motivating targeted
DFT-informed fine-tuning before quantitative yield prediction.
